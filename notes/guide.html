Welcome to FX Journal — Your Complete Trading Companion

This comprehensive guide will walk you through every feature of FX Journal V2. Learn how to log trades, analyze performance, use our powerful tools, and ultimately improve your trading results through disciplined journaling.

Pro Tip: Consistent journaling is the #1 habit of successful traders. Spend 10 minutes after each trade documenting your thoughts and review your journal weekly.
🚀 Getting Started
1
Set Up Your Account
Go to Settings and enter your trader name, strategy/style, timezone, and preferred currency. This personalizes your journal and ensures accurate time tracking for all your trades.
2
Create Your First Account
Navigate to the Accounts section and add your trading account(s). You can track multiple accounts including live, demo, and prop firm accounts. Set starting balance, broker info, and any prop firm rules like daily drawdown limits.
3
Configure Your Rules
In the Rules section, set your risk management rules: max daily loss, max trades per day, minimum R:R ratio, and allowed instruments. These rules will warn you when violated, helping maintain discipline.
💡
Start Small
Don't feel overwhelmed by all features. Start by logging your next 5 trades with basic info (pair, direction, P&L). Gradually add more details as you get comfortable with the system.
📝 Logging Trades Effectively
➕
Quick Add
Click "+ Trade" button anywhere to quickly log a completed trade. Fill in essential fields first, then add details.
📊
Auto Calculations
P&L and R:R are automatically calculated from entry, exit, SL, and lot size. Just enter your prices!
🎯
Grade Your Trades
Rate each trade A+ to C based on execution quality, not outcome. A losing trade can be an A if well-executed.
😌
Track Emotions
Select emotions felt during the trade (Calm, FOMO, Confident, etc). Identify emotional patterns over time.
📸
Add Screenshots
Upload chart screenshots showing entry, exit, and analysis. Visual review accelerates learning.
✅
Pre-Trade Checklist
Complete your custom checklist before entering. Ensures you follow your process every time.
🎯
The 5-Minute Journal Routine
Before Trade: Check your pre-trade checklist, note setup type and confluences.
After Trade: Log results immediately while fresh. Add screenshot, grade execution, note emotions.
Weekly Review: Every weekend, review all trades from the week. What patterns do you see?
📈 Mastering the Analytics Dashboard
1
Overview Tab
Your trading command center. Shows equity curve, win/loss distribution, key statistics (win rate, profit factor, expectancy), and performance by day of week. Use this for your weekly reviews.
2
Pairs Analysis
See which currency pairs are most profitable for you. Focus on your best performers and consider reducing trades on underperforming pairs.
3
Session Performance
Discover which trading sessions (London, NY, Tokyo, Sydney) work best for your style. Many traders find they perform better during specific sessions.
4
Setup Analysis
Track performance by setup type (breakout, pullback, reversal, etc). Double down on what works, eliminate what doesn't.
5
Emotion Tracking
Correlate emotions with performance. Do you lose more when anxious? Win more when patient? This insight is gold for improvement.
6
Calendar View
Visual overview of trading days. Click any day to see all trades. Great for spotting overtrading or inconsistent activity patterns.
📊
Key Metrics to Watch
Profit Factor: Aim for 1.5+. Gross wins ÷ gross losses.
Win Rate: 40-60% is typical. Higher isn't always better if R:R suffers.
Average R:R: Target 1:2 or better. Risk $1 to make $2+.
Expectancy: Average $ won per trade. Positive = edge.
📒 Deep Dive: Journal Section

The Journal section is where the magic happens. This is your trading diary — a place for deep reflection and continuous improvement.

1
Access Your Trades
Left panel shows all trades. Filter by date range or status (All, Pending Journal, Completed). Click any trade to open journal view.
2
Write Quality Analysis
Don't just write "good trade" or "bad trade". Be specific: What was your thesis? What worked? What didn't? Would you take this trade again? Why or why not?
3
Link Related Trades
If you took multiple trades on the same setup or during the same session, link them together. Identify series and streaks.
4
Create Notes & Folders
Use the Notes section to document strategies, market observations, lessons learned. Organize with folders like "Price Action", "ICT Concepts", "Psychology Notes".
✍️
Journal Prompts for Better Reflection
• What was my emotional state before/during/after this trade?
• Did I follow my trading plan exactly? If not, why?
• What would I do differently if I could replay this trade?
• What does this trade teach me about my strengths/weaknesses?
• Is this trade part of a pattern I should be aware of?
🛠️ Trading Tools Suite
📐
Position Size Calculator
Calculate perfect position size based on account balance, risk %, and stop loss. Supports forex majors, XAUUSD, XAGUSD, BTCUSD, ETHUSD. Shows pip calculation for each instrument.
📊
Gain/Loss Calculator
Project potential profit/loss before entering. Enter entry, target, position size. See gross P&L, commission impact, and return percentage.
🌍
Market Hours Tracker
Real-time session tracker for Sydney, Tokyo, London, NY. Auto-adjusts for DST. Select your timezone. Shows overlaps (high liquidity periods).
🎯
Daily Tool Workflow
Before Trading: Check Market Hours to know which sessions are active. Use Gain/Loss Calculator to plan your trade.
During Setup: Use Position Calculator to determine exact lot size based on your stop loss distance.
Always: Never guess position sizes. Always calculate based on risk, not gut feeling.

⭐ Best Practices for Maximum Results
📅
Daily Routine
Log every trade same day. Review yesterday's trades each morning. Plan today's levels and setups.
📆
Weekly Review
Every weekend: Review all trades, check analytics, update notes, adjust rules if needed.
📊
Monthly Analysis
Deep dive into monthly stats. Compare to previous months. Set goals for next month.
💾
Backup Regularly
Download JSON backup weekly. Store in cloud drive. Never lose your valuable data.
🎯
Focus on Process
Grade trades on execution, not outcome. Good process + time = profitability.
🧠
Track Psychology
Your emotions and mindset matter more than indicators. Track them religiously.
🏆
The Path to Consistency
Month 1-2: Focus on logging every trade completely. Build the habit.
Month 3-4: Start reviewing analytics weekly. Identify 1-2 areas to improve.
Month 5-6: Refine your edge based on data. Eliminate low-probability setups.
Month 6+: You'll see clear patterns, know your edge, and trade with confidence backed by data.
