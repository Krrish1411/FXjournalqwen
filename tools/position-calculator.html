Position Size Calculator

Calculate the perfect position size for your trades based on your risk tolerance. This tool helps you manage risk properly by determining how many lots/units to trade based on your account size, risk percentage, and stop loss distance.

Why use this? Proper position sizing is crucial for long-term trading success. It ensures you never risk too much on a single trade and helps maintain consistent risk management across all your trades.

Position Sizing Formula
