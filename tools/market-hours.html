Forex Market Hours Tracker

Real-time tracking of major forex trading sessions (Sydney, Tokyo, London, New York) with automatic daylight saving time adjustments. See which sessions are currently open and plan your trades accordingly.

Why use this? Different currency pairs are most active during specific sessions. Knowing when markets are open helps you trade during high liquidity periods and avoid low-volume times that can lead to slippage.
⏰
Automatic Daylight Saving Time Adjustment
All session times automatically adjust for DST changes in each region. London switches to BST, Sydney to AEDT, and New York to EDT when applicable.
